The Cold Open, Act Two

The cold open, act two

Not a carousel. The opening you already have, carried two seconds further — so the first thing a new hopper sees is the app doing its trick, rather than a card describing it.

PONDHOPPING

Your photos already know
where you went.

The sequence
0.0sThe sphere draws itself
0.5sA route crosses it
0.6sThe duck flies the route and lands; the water rings out
1.6sPond Hopping resolves
2.3sThree photographs drop onto the globe
3.3sEach one folds down onto the place it was taken
4.1sA line joins them — the trip, made out of the pile
4.4sOne sentence, and then the app

Why this instead of a card. The card asks to be read and then dismissed; nobody arrives wanting to read. This says the same thing in the medium the claim is actually about — a pile of photographs becoming a journey — and it ends by putting them in the app rather than in front of a button.

What it costs. Two more seconds, on the first launch only, and it replaces the intro card entirely — so the first run gets shorter, not longer. Everything moving is opacity, transform or stroke-dashoffset, the three things a compositor runs on its own thread. Same budget the current opening is built to, which is why it holds sixty frames on an old phone.

The one risk. Five seconds is a long splash if it ever runs twice. It must stay welded to the same first-run flag as the current cold open — seen once, ever, and never again.
