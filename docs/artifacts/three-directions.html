Direction 01

Brasswork — editorial luxury

Warm charcoal ground, aged brass and a single oxblood ember. A high-contrast italic serif carries the name like a maker's mark; a fine-line crest sits low in the frame, half in shadow. The mood of a private members' club rather than a tech portfolio.

Porcelain & Ink — quiet confidence
