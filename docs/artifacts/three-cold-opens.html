What each would cost. All three are hand-written SVG and CSS — the same technique that's on production now, so no new dependency, nothing to install, and they weigh about as much as this paragraph. A day's work each, give or take.

  • None of them show a real trip. No dates, no distances, nobody's holiday. That's the point — but it does mean losing the card-morphs-into-the-Home-screen handover, unless we land on the "Tip it in" tile instead, which is now the hero for anyone with an empty globe.
  • Mix and match is fine. A's opening pile with B's accelerating arcs is probably the strongest single thing here, and it's a sentence to say so.
  • These are rough. Timing, easing and the drawing itself all get another pass once there's a direction to polish toward.
