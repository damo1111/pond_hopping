Three things I need from you

1. Two of your numbers don't exist. This trip has no photographs at all — not one — and there is no step count anywhere in the schema. So "300 pics" and "15k steps" would be invented, and the whole rule is that the opening cannot show something the app doesn't make. What it does have is above, and it is plenty.

2. That absence is your best argument. The richest trip in the app is made entirely of flights, runs and things you wrote. "It all starts with a photo, or a run, or..." isn't a nice list — it is literally how this trip exists. If steps ever arrive as a source, the line already has room for them.

3. It is now 900ms longer, and I said it wouldn't be. Six figures at 150ms each cost most of it. Either accept 7.2s, drop to four figures, or shorten the held beat. Watch it at ¼ speed and tell me which — my instinct is to cut 128 km run and 16 written and keep the four that land hardest.

4. The line is still mine. It should be yours. That sentence is the most important one in the app.

What I'd flag before building it for real

The current opening ends on “Your photos already know where you went” — one source, stated as the whole story. That's the line this replaces, and replacing it is most of the point: anyone whose trip isn't photographed currently hears this isn't for you.

It also only ever runs once, gated on cold_open in firstRun.js. Everything above is one launch in somebody's life, which is the argument both for making it good and for not making it longer.
